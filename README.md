🌟 About FreeSite

FreeSite is a modern Multi-Tenant SaaS platform designed to combine website integration, AI-powered customer assistance, and WhatsApp automation into one centralized ecosystem.

Users can connect their websites, configure personalized AI assistants, manage product catalogs, automate customer conversations, and control their services through a centralized dashboard.

Our mission is to make website management and business automation easier, smarter, and more accessible.

🏗️ Platform Architecture

FreeSite is designed around a modular architecture.

flowchart TD
    A[User Dashboard] --> B[Backend API]
    B --> C[Authentication]
    B --> D[Website Integration]
    B --> E[AI Engine]
    B --> F[Subscription System]
    D --> G[Product Catalog]
    E --> H[WhatsApp Automation]
    G --> H
    B --> I[Admin Dashboard]
    B --> J[Database]
    B --> K[Cloud Storage]
    B --> L[Webhook Dispatcher]

Platform Workflow

1. User creates an account.
2. User connects a website through its URL.
3. The system analyzes the website.
4. Product information is collected where accessible.
5. User configures their AI assistant.
6. User connects a WhatsApp session.
7. AI assistant responds using synchronized product information.
8. User manages everything through the dashboard.
